## Hypotheses (ranked)
1. (~70 %) Resume from s2idle (triggered by the wake source) hits a fatal fault. Firmware (TZ/Gunyah) then issues a PS_HOLD warm reset:
   a watchdog bite after resume, or a NoC/XPU/secure fault from a device resume callback.
   Supporting evidence: wake-coincident timing on both deaths; warm (not hard) reset, while Linux's own reboot path gives hard resets;
   cpuidle s1/s2 off did not help, so it is not the CPU/cluster power-collapse idle path.
2. (~20 %) Linux itself panics on resume and restarts. kernel.panic=10 is still set; panic_on_oops and the lockup panics are 0.
   This would need the restart path to be warm, which does not match the clean reboots.
3. (<10 %) Power/battery (UVLO, brownout): ruled out by the PON log. There is no OFF state or fault, and it happened at 89–96 % too.
